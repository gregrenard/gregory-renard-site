SOLUTION STAGE · GENEVA · 09 JUL 2026
The talk, in four beats

Generative AI is now in children's lives. It still has no seatbelt.

01 The third way

A developmental seatbelt

Not banning AI, not unfettered innovation — a reasonable, auditable standard for every AI tool a child uses. Volvo gave cars a seatbelt in 1959. Generative AI is now in children’s lives, with no seatbelt yet.

02 What we measure

15 behaviors, 3 domain cues

Anthropomorphic, Interactional and Relational cues, each scored on a 5-level gradient. Our CALIBER method turns developmental science into a scalable, auditable behavioral assessment for AI.

03 The tool

AiA Safety Builder

A pipeline — Endpoints → Attack → Content Gate → Score + Report → Guardrails. It diagnoses risky behaviors and auto-generates measured guardrails you can audit.

04 The goal

A safer place for children

Give every family a standard they can trust, and every builder a measurable path to it. Beneficial AI for children — defined, scored, and scaled.

Let's make AI a safer place for children.
